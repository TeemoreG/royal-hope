About Royal Hope Home Care

Bringing hospital-quality healthcare to the comfort of your home — with compassion, expertise, and dignity.

Who We Are

Royal Hope Home Based Care was founded on one belief: that every Kenyan family deserves professional, hospital-quality medical care — delivered with warmth, right in their own home.

Our team of trained, NCK-certified nurses and caregivers come directly to your door. Whether your loved one is recovering from surgery, managing a chronic illness, needs elderly daily support, or requires postnatal care — we are there.

We serve all ages across Nairobi and Kiambu County, working closely with your doctors and hospitals to ensure continuity of care at every step.
