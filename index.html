Who We Are

Bringing Royal Care to Every Home in Nairobi

RoyalHope Home Based Care was founded to make professional, hospital-quality medical care available to every Kenyan family — delivered to the comfort of their own home.

Healing happens best in familiar surroundings. Our team of trained, certified nurses and caregivers come directly to your door — whether recovering from surgery, managing a chronic illness, or supporting an elderly parent.

We serve all ages across Nairobi and Kiambu County, coordinating closely with your doctors and hospitals throughout.
