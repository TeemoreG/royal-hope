Who We Are

Bringing Royal Care to Every Home in Kenya

RoyalHope Home Based Care was founded with a clear mission: to make professional, hospital-quality medical care available to every Kenyan family — delivered right to the comfort and safety of their own home.

We understand that hospitals can feel overwhelming and impersonal. Healing happens best in familiar surroundings, with family nearby. That's why we send our team of trained, certified nurses and caregivers directly to your door — whether you're recovering from surgery, managing a chronic illness, or caring for an elderly parent.

Our care is safe, professional, and deeply compassionate. We serve patients of all ages across Nairobi and beyond, and we coordinate closely with your existing doctors and hospitals.

🏠 What We Offer

Our Medical Services

Every service is tailored to your loved one's specific needs — delivered professionally and compassionately in the comfort of home.

🩺

Home Nursing Care

Registered nurses visit your home for medical assessments, treatment, wound care, injections, and ongoing health monitoring — all in the comfort of home.

👴

Elderly Care & Daily Support

Compassionate daily assistance — mobility support, personal hygiene, meal preparation, medication reminders, and meaningful companionship for elderly patients.

💊

Chronic Illness Management

Structured ongoing care for patients with diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, kidney disease, and other long-term conditions — keeping symptoms under control.

🩹

Wound Dressing & Catheter Care

Sterile, professional wound cleaning, dressing changes, and catheter management done safely and hygienically in your home to prevent infection and promote healing.

🫀

Dialysis Follow-up & AV Fistula Care

Dedicated home monitoring and AV fistula care for dialysis patients between sessions — ensuring vascular access health and complication prevention.

🏥

Post-Hospital Discharge Care

Seamless recovery care after discharge — reducing re-admission risk and ensuring safe healing at home with professional nursing support and medication management.

💉

Medication Administration

Accurate, on-schedule administration of prescribed medications — injections, IV therapy, oral medications — with monitoring for side effects and patient response.

🕊️

Palliative & Supportive Care

Dignified, compassionate end-of-life care focused on comfort, effective pain management, and emotional support for both patients and their families.

📊

Vital Signs Monitoring

Regular, accurate measurement of blood pressure, temperature, pulse, oxygen saturation, and blood glucose — with timely reporting to your doctor when needed.
